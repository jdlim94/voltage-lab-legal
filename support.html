What Voltage Lab is

A revision app for O-Level, GCSE and IGCSE physics, starting with electricity. Each lesson is built around a diagram you can drive — move a slider or flip a cell and the current, voltage and meter readings all respond — followed by exam-style questions with the working shown.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Open the app and start.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Every lesson and question is inside the app, so it works on a bus, on a plane, or anywhere with no signal.

Where is my progress stored?

On your device only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

I deleted the app. Can I get my streak back?

Unfortunately not. Because progress is stored only on your device and never sent to a server, uninstalling removes it permanently. There is no copy to restore from.

What is in the app right now?

Four lessons covering charge and current, e.m.f. and potential difference, resistance, and I-V characteristics — 84 questions in total.

When are the other topics coming?

The full O-Level physics roadmap is listed inside the app under Subjects — measurement, kinematics, dynamics, energy, thermal physics, waves, light and magnetism. They are being written and will arrive in future updates.

A question looks wrong to me

Please tell me. Getting a mark scheme wrong in a revision app is the worst kind of bug, and I would rather hear about it than not. Email the address above.

Reporting a problem

If the app crashes or behaves oddly, it helps to include:

  • Your device (for example, iPhone 14) and iOS version
  • What you were doing when it happened
  • A screenshot, if you have one
