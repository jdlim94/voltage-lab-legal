Privacy Policy — Voltage Lab

Last updated 20 August 2026

Voltage Lab does not collect any personal data. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Everything. Your progress — lessons completed, XP, streaks, which questions you have answered and how you answered them — is stored only in the app's own storage on your iPhone. We never receive a copy of it.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no server-side record to request or erase, because none was ever created.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts. This version offers no sign-up or log-in of any kind.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting software.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no ad identifiers are read.
  • No third-party data sharing. We have no data to share.
  • No microphone or camera access. The app plays short sound effects for correct and incorrect answers; it never records anything.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or health data.

Questions and lesson content

The lessons and question banks are included inside the app when you download it. Using them requires no internet connection and generates no network requests to us.

Sharing

If you choose to tap a share button — for example to share your streak — iOS opens its own standard share sheet and you decide where the text goes. We do not see, receive, or store anything you share, and nothing is shared unless you actively choose to.

Children's privacy

Voltage Lab is a revision aid for secondary-school physics, so some users will be children. Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, it collects none from children either. We do not knowingly or unknowingly gather data from users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app introduces accounts or any other feature that handles personal data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
